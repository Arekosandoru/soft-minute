Affirmation Journey Support

Affirmation Journey is a calm affirmation app for iPhone and Android: a daily feed of affirmations, short timed practices, reminders, and challenges.

Contact

Email: coming soon. In the app you can also use Settings → Contact Support.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Premium is billed by Apple or Google. To change or cancel it:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Affirmation Journey.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Affirmation Journey.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Restore Premium on a new device

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account, then open Settings → Restore subscription in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully as a guest, with your progress stored on your device. An account keeps your progress in sync across devices.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications for Affirmation Journey are allowed in your phone's settings and that reminders are turned on in Settings → Reminders. On Android a daily reminder may arrive up to an hour after the chosen time.
