Delete your Soft Minute account

This page explains how to delete your Soft Minute account and the data linked to it. You do not need to have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Open Soft Minute and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Your account and its data are deleted from our servers right away.

Without the app

Email us from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, write from the Apple ID email and mention that you used Sign in with Apple. We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

Email: coming soon.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address or Apple/Google sign-in identifier, and password hash.
  • Your settings: chosen topics, theme, and reminder status.
  • Favorite affirmations, challenge progress, practice sessions, and check-ins.
  • Sign-in tokens.

What is kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations.
  • Crash reports in Sentry do not include your name or email and are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings; see Support.

Guest data

If you used the app without an account, your data is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
