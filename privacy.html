Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Affirmation Journey collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use Affirmation Journey as a guest. Your preferences, favorites, practice progress, check-ins, and reminder settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

2. If you create an account

We store your name and email address, or the identifier provided by Apple or Google sign-in. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.

3. What syncs to your account

When you are signed in, we store on our servers: your chosen topics, theme, and onboarding status; whether reminders are turned on; your favorite affirmations; challenge progress; practice sessions (date, length, and number of affirmations); and short ritual check-ins (the mood you pick from a list before and after practice). We use this only to keep your account the same across devices.

4. Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID or your account ID and your purchase details. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your account has Premium.

5. Crash reports

We use Sentry to receive crash and error reports. These include technical details such as the error, app version, and device model. We do not attach your name or email to them.

6. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not send push notifications and do not collect a push token.

7. Images and sharing

Theme images are built into the app. When you share an affirmation, the image is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.

8. What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads, use advertising or third-party analytics tools, or track you across other apps and websites.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, favorites, challenge progress, practice sessions, check-ins, and sign-in tokens from our servers. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies. To cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion. For any other request, contact support.

10. Children

Affirmation Journey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
